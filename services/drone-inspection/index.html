End-to-End Inspections Without Shutdowns

FEDS inspects live assets, like flare stacks, powerlines, and chimneys, without interrupting operations. Using advanced sensors and certified pilots, we capture detailed data remotely while eliminating safety risks and costly downtime. Our UAV Inspection technology ensures accurate, real-time results.

AI-Enabled Anomaly Detection and Reporting

Captured data is processed using AI models trained to identify structural risks, electrical faults, corrosion, and other anomalies. We deliver annotated visuals and actionable insights that help you prioritise maintenance, reduce rework, and improve compliance.

Access to Confined, Hazardous, and Underwater Spaces

From confined tanks to underwater foundations, our specialised drones and ROVs reach places where humans can’t. Every mission is precisely planned and remotely executed to protect your teams and minimise permit overhead.

Multi-Sensor Intelligence From a Single Deployment

Our drones can be equipped with thermal, UV, gas, RGB, GPR, and medium-format sensors, enabling you to gather comprehensive asset data in one go. No need to coordinate multiple teams or tools. The use of drone sensor technology guarantees clarity and consistency.
