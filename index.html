FAQs

Comprehensive briefing on our autonomous drone inspection solutions, deep-learning computer vision models, radiometric thermography, and CMMS integration across power and renewable energy infrastructure.

explore platform & demo
1. What critical power and energy assets does NEXUS OS inspect?

NEXUS OS provides autonomous inspection and computer vision defect detection across high-voltage overhead transmission lines (OHTL), utility-scale solar photovoltaic farms, onshore and offshore wind turbines, thermal and combined-cycle power facilities, and hydroelectric dam structures.

2. What accuracy and defect classification benchmarks does the platform achieve?

Our deep learning computer vision models achieve sub-millimeter defect detection and over 97.8% mAP@50 across standard IEEE and IEC defect taxonomies, identifying missing cotter pins, insulator flashovers, conductor strand breaks, PV cell microcracks, and wind blade structural delamination.

3. How does radiometric thermography work for utility-scale solar farms?

Compliant with IEC 62446-3 thermographic inspection standards, our drone payloads capture calibrated radiometric thermal data and high-resolution RGB imagery. Deep-learning algorithms automatically pinpoint cell hot spots, bypass diode failures, PID degradation, and string dropouts with exact GPS coordinates and kilowatt loss estimations.

4. Which drone platforms and inspection sensors are supported?

The platform ingests multimodal telemetry from industry-standard enterprise UAVs, including DJI Matrice 300 and 350 RTK, Zenmuse H20T and H30T, Zenmuse P1, L2 LiDAR, Phase One medium-format payloads, and FLIR radiometric thermal systems.

5. How does NEXUS OS integrate with enterprise CMMS and ERP workflows?

NEXUS OS features native bi-directional APIs for SAP Plant Maintenance (PM), IBM Maximo, Oracle Primavera, and GIS GeoJSON environments. Inspection anomalies automatically generate prioritized work orders with geolocated defect coordinates and repair priority scores.

6. Can the platform be deployed on-premise or in air-gapped environments?

Yes. In addition to sovereign enterprise cloud hosting, NEXUS OS supports fully on-premise and air-gapped deployments on certified secure clusters for critical national infrastructure and utility grid compliance.

7. How does autonomous drone inspection compare to traditional manual auditing?

Autonomous drone inspection eliminates life-threatening rope access and helicopter flybys, reduces inspection costs by over 80%, accelerates corridor turnaround by 10x, and inspects fully energized high-voltage lines without requiring operational shutdowns.

8. How are 3D digital twins and corridor maps created?

Raw drone LiDAR and photogrammetry datasets are processed into millimeter-accurate 3D point clouds and georeferenced orthomosaics. Defect annotations are pinned in true 3D spatial coordinates for historical degradation tracking along the P-F curve.

9. What is the typical turnaround time from drone flight to inspection reporting?

Flight data uploaded to NEXUS OS is processed through automated computer vision pipelines within 24 to 48 hours, delivering verified defect classifications, interactive digital twins, and CMMS-ready work order packages.

10. Does NEXUS OS provide turnkey Drone Inspection-as-a-Service (IaaS)?

Yes. We offer turnkey Inspection-as-a-Service (IaaS) including certified flight crews and regulatory compliance, as well as software-only enterprise SaaS licenses for organizations with in-house drone operations teams.
