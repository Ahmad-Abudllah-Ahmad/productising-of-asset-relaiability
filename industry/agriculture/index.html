Everything You Need to Monitor Agricultural Land - from the air, in-house, and on time

Built for teams who need reliable data, not contractor complexity. FEDS combines precision agricultural drone surveys, in-house data processing, and AI-powered analysis to provide complete visibility into land use and crop health.

01

Large-Scale Farmland Surveys, Completed in Days

We execute missions across hundreds and thousands of square kilometers with minimal personnel. From permits to flight plans and execution, everything is handled by our team, allowing you to inspect more land in less time.

02

Aerial Data That Flags Misuse, Violations, and Risk Areas

Our drones for crop inspection use NDVI, RGB, and thermal sensors to capture detailed imagery of farmland. This data reveals issues like poor irrigation, illegal waste burning, unapproved boundary changes, pest infestation, and compliance violations. All visible in a single aerial layer.

03

AI-Powered Classification for Faster, Smarter Decision-Making

Once collected, data is processed using customised AI models we’ve trained and fine-tuned with our own regional datasets and quality controls. The result: clear insights on crop health, land use patterns, and compliance risks. No raw footage. No guesswork. Just actionable outputs.

04

Unlike many providers, FEDS doesn’t outsource or subcontract any part of the workflow. All data collection, processing, and reporting is done by our internal team. Mapping 1,100 km² of Farmland

CLIENT

Ministry of Climate Change & Environment (MoCCaE)

THE CHALLENGE

Map 1,100 km² of agricultural land fast enough that the information wouldn’t go stale while also accounting for livestock (including camels), trees, wells, pipelines, and structures. Satellite imagery lacked the needed clarity, and terrestrial surveys were too slow and costly.

FEDS SOLUTIONS

Deployed senseFly eBeeX fixed-wing drones to survey across six emirates and completed data capture in 64 working days (about 17 km² per day). Built and trained a semi-supervised ML model—validated with human QC—to identify and classify flora, fauna, and key assets from orthomosaics.

Results
Delivered orthomosaic maps and asset-survey reports covering 1,100 km² in 64 working days.
Automated, validated counts and classifications of trees (by age), livestock, greenhouses, wells, and pipelines.
Actionable insights helped MoCCaE guide farmers on resource needs and crop choices for sustainable planning.
